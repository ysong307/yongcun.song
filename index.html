About

Research at the intersection of mathematical analysis, computation, and learning.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Division of Mathematical Sciences at Nanyang Technological University.

My work centers on scientific computing, machine learning, optimal control, and optimization. I obtained my PhD from The University of Hong Kong in 2021. From 2021 to 2024, I was a postdoctoral researcher and an Alexander von Humboldt Research Fellow at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg, Germany.

Before joining NTU, I served as an Assistant Professor (tenure-track) at City University of Hong Kong from September 2024 to August 2025.

Research

A computational view of complex mathematical problems.

I develop and analyse methods that combine optimization, control, numerical computation, and modern learning techniques. Select an area to see its current research scope.

Click a research area to reveal its themes and related record.

Scientific Computing

Reliable computation for complex systems.

Numerical analysis and computational methods for models governed by partial differential equations and dynamical systems.

  • Optimal control of PDEs and control of dynamical systems
  • Inverse problems
  • Shape design
  • Physics simulations
View scientific computing publications →

Machine Learning

Learning methods with mathematical structure.

Data-driven and scientifically informed learning methods, together with the analysis needed to make them reliable for computation.

  • Scientific machine learning: physics-informed learning, optimization-informed neural networks, and operator learning
  • Mathematics of machine learning: approximation and generalization theory, convergence, and error estimates for SciML
  • Federated learning
  • Control and machine learning
View machine-learning publications →

Optimal Control

From PDE models to computable decisions.

Methods for the analysis and numerical solution of constrained control problems, with an emphasis on PDEs, dynamics, and learning-enabled algorithms.

  • PDE-constrained optimal control
  • Control of dynamical systems
  • State- and control-constrained problems
  • Learning and operator-learning methods for nonsmooth PDE control
Explore the optimal-control research path →

Optimization

Algorithms for structured mathematical problems.

Optimization theory and algorithms for large-scale, nonsmooth, and constrained problems arising in computation, control, and learning.

  • Stochastic optimization for large-scale nonsmooth problems
  • Operator splitting methods
  • Primal-dual and ADMM-type algorithms
  • Proximal and quasi-Newton methods
