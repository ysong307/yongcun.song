Research Interests

Mathematical structure, computation, and learning.

The homepage presents four complementary paths through the research programme. The detailed public interests below retain the substance of the existing research record, while Optimal Control is shown independently as a central cross-cutting theme.

01 / Scientific Computing

Scientific Computing

  • Optimal control of PDEs and control of dynamical systems
  • Inverse problems
  • Shape design
  • Physics simulations
02 / Machine Learning

Machine Learning

  • Scientific machine learning: physics-informed, optimization-informed, and operator learning
  • Approximation and generalization theory; convergence and error estimates for SciML
  • Federated learning
  • Control and machine learning
03 / Optimal Control

Optimal Control

  • PDE-constrained optimal control
  • Control of dynamical systems
  • State- and control-constrained problems
  • Learning-enabled and operator-learning control methods
04 / Optimization

Optimization

  • Stochastic optimization
  • Operator splitting methods
  • Primal-dual and ADMM-type algorithms
  • Proximal and quasi-Newton methods

How the record is organised. Publications are kept in the four collections used by the original research record: Scientific Computing, Optimization, Scientific Machine Learning, and Machine Learning. Individual papers may naturally connect more than one research interest.
